# Agent-Native Research Artifact

## Project

Offline Vietnamese clinical entity extraction and coding for Viettel AI Race V1.

## Layers

- `logic/`: problem, claims, and solution rationale.
- `trace/`: exploration history and session provenance.
- `evidence/`: quantitative and external evidence references.
- `staging/`: observations not yet promoted.

External leaderboard evidence and locally reconstructed metrics are deliberately
kept distinct.
