# H43 analysis — rejected prompt-instability surgery

The complete 621-tuple dual review was internally deterministic: both frozen
seeds agreed on every KEEP/DROP action and proposed 79 DROP tuples affecting 121
candidate uses. After safe-alias and unknown-title controls, the transform would
remove 118 uses while retaining all 102 curated positive-control uses.

H43 nevertheless fails its most important independent stability gate. Only one
of 19 H38 uses belonging to known-title contradictions from the frozen earlier
review was removed, a 5.26% rate versus the registered 80% minimum. Agreement
between two seeds in one prompt run is therefore not evidence of stable semantic
calibration across time/context batches.

The candidate ZIP was deleted automatically. No submission is authorized from
H43. This negative result motivates deterministic hierarchy rules whose mechanism
has already been externally measured.
