What changed

H48's qualitative lesson survives; its numerical recommendation does not.

Fifteen passages remain underpowered. But 30 passages do not robustly reach 80% at a moderate effect, and the existing percentile gate can be anti-conservative under left skew.

Decision

Do not open labels into an uncalibrated gate.

Keep H41 frozen. Preregister a small-sample inference repair and evaluate it against the same five-family envelope. Until then, report the current lower bound as distribution-sensitive, never as a universal 5% test or evidence of equivalence.
