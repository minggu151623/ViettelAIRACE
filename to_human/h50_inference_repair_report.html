The tradeoff is structural

Resampling cannot manufacture independent information.

The existing method promotes too often under left skew. Studentization corrects that asymmetry by widening the bound, but 15 independent passages do not contain enough information to preserve useful moderate-effect power. Discrete ties additionally destabilize bootstrap-t.

What H41 is still good for

A universal 5% promotion test, equivalence after non-promotion, or robust detection of moderate effects from the 15-passage holdout.

Evaluator unchanged; confirmatory promotion remains blocked.
